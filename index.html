Welcome to Ocean Gardens

Ocean Gardens is dedicated to coral enthusiasts of all experience levels. This site shares helpful information about coral care, reef aquarium maintenance, and responsible husbandry. With proper lighting, water flow, stable water chemistry, and patience, keeping corals can be one of the most rewarding aquarium hobbies.
